GPIO 34 (ADC1_CH6) – Solar Voltage Sensor

Connects to a precision 1% tolerance resistor voltage divider (e.g. 100kΩ / 10kΩ) to scale high solar panel voltages (0–30V DC) into the ESP32 ADC safe range (0–3.3V DC).

• Expected Input: 0.0V to 3.1V DC
• ADC Resolution: 12-bit (0–4095 levels, 0.8mV/step)
• Protection: 3.3V Zener diode clamp recommended
• Multimeter Check: Probe junction at TP-SOLAR-V

Frequently Asked Questions
How often does the ESP32 broadcast telemetry? ▼
The ESP32 broadcasts JSON payloads every 1,000ms (1 second) via HTTP POST to /api/telemetry. The dashboard polls /api/data at 1-second intervals for real-time visualization.
How is Solar Panel Efficiency calculated? ▼
Efficiency is computed as: (Generated Power (W) / (Solar Surface Area (m²) × Irradiance (W/m²))) × 100%. Modern monocrystalline panels operate nominally between 17% and 22%.
What happens if local Wi-Fi goes down? ▼
The ESP32 stores telemetry packets in its internal SPIFFS flash buffer. Once the local access point is restored, cached records are synced without losing yield totals.
